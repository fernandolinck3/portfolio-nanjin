## Rejected candidates

Two images from the `engravings` are.na board were checked and **cannot be used**:

- **"Atlas Coelestis: Hemisphaeria Boreale et Australe"** — carries a Dreamstime watermark. A modern
  stock illustration imitating this genre, not a historical plate. `plate.jpg` above is the genuine
  article it was imitating.
- **"The Zodiac / 天星秘语"** — a contemporary illustration signed "Infinite Night". Copyrighted.

The **Calendarium Perpetuum** from the same board *is* genuine — a 17th-century Nuremberg
broadsheet, public domain — and is a good candidate for the Deck faces, since a volvelle is
literally a jog wheel. Not yet sourced at high resolution.
