# Plate artwork

Two paths, checked in this order:

## 1. `plate.png` / `plate.jpg` / `plate.svg` — the OBNE approach

One full-bleed painting or engraving covering the whole Plate, with the control panel printed over
it. This is the resolving idea from the brief: a working control panel silkscreened over a woodcut.

- **Landscape, 5.6 : 3.28 or wider.** It is cover-fitted, so a portrait image gets cropped hard.
- **2048px wide or more.**
- **High contrast, limited palette.** It has to survive a dark scrim and still read under the Print.
- The `SCRIM` dial controls how far it is knocked back so labels stay legible.
- The artwork also drives a shallow relief, so it reads as printed on textured metal rather than as
  a flat sticker.
- With plate art present the ornamental frame switches itself off — OBNE panels carry no border.

## 2. `pattern.svg` / `pattern.png` — tiled ornament

A repeating unit tiled across the Plate. Solid shapes, not strokes-only; black on transparent.
`TILE` controls the repeat.

If neither is present the procedural vine runs as a placeholder.
