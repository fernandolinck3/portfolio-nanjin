# The Unit sits on an altar, and the camera tilts to see it

The Unit rests on a black marble slab under an embroidered cloth, with three gilt candlesticks
standing around it. The three lights that were previously invisible directionals now have bodies:
each Candle carries a flame and its own light, and they are extinguished one at a time as the Vigil
rises until only the Screen burns.

Without a setting the Vigil dimmed an abstraction — there was nothing in frame to lose. With one it
performs the rite the object is named after. The register is deliberately Baroque and liturgical
rather than occult-satanic: polished marble, gilt, linen, warm candlelight and deep shadow. The
lighting model is tenebrism, the Baroque technique that shares the name's root.

**This tilts the camera off vertical, which qualifies ADR-0007.** A candlestick seen from directly
above is a brass disc; it only reads as a candlestick in profile. The base angle is now about 30°
rather than near-vertical, and it is a tunable (`CAM`). ADR-0007 still holds where it matters: there
is no orbit, no inspect mode, and the Unit's own tilt stays bounded to a few degrees of damped
pointer lean.

**Cost**: the Plate is foreshortened, so the faceplate art reads less directly than it did straight
down, and the Parts were designed against a top-down composition. That trade was accepted because
the Vigil is the object's central mechanic and it did not previously land.
