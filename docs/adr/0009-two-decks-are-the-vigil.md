# Two Decks, Sun and Moon, are the Vigil

The Unit has two wheels flanking a centred Screen — Moon on the left, Sun on the right — and there
is no Vigil knob. Turning the Sun brings the light up; turning the Moon puts it out. The rite the
model name comes from, performed with two hands.

This replaces a single jog wheel plus a rotary Vigil control, and it reverses the earlier decision
that the jog digs through the live Module's contents. Navigation is now carried by the six Pads
alone.

**Consequences**: every Module must fit the Screen exactly, because nothing scrolls and no control
browses. The Plate widened to 5.6 × 3.28 to seat two decks, which moved the Screen to the centre and
the Pads and Crossfader into the column beneath it. The Sun/Moon pairing also gives the Vigil a face:
the light is depicted on the two Parts that control it.

**Rejected alternatives**: Sun as a Now deck and Moon as a Next deck, with the Crossfader between
them — structurally elegant and it would have made the Crossfader literally true, but it demanded a
restructure of the six-Module content model. And Sun-browses / Moon-digs, which kept every earlier
decision intact but spent both wheels on navigation and left the Sun/Moon pairing purely decorative.
