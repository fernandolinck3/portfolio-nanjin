# The Crossfader moves; the honest position is engraved

The Now↔Next crossfader is draggable and re-weights what the Screen shows, but an index mark at the
honest position is engraved into the Plate and cannot move. Copy on the Next side is future-tense
throughout — learning, building, dated — never claimed experience.

`PRODUCT.md` prohibits presenting AI, automation and analytics as established professional
experience. A freely draggable fader could otherwise let a visitor push the Unit into making that
exact claim. Welding the fader shut was rejected because the Unit promises that every control does
something real.

**Consequences**: the immovable index mark is load-bearing, not decoration — do not make it track
the fader. This is the same rule the ornament follows: ornament may overgrow, labels never move.
