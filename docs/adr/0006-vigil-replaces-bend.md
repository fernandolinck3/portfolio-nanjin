# Vigil (light) replaces the Bend (corruption)

The prototype's knob drove a "bend" that melted the chassis toward chrome and foliated the tracery —
corruption for its own sake. It is removed. The knob is now VIGIL, a light control: turning it up
extinguishes the Unit's lamps one at a time until the Screen's phosphor is the only source in the
room, and the raking light reveals the Nightwork engraved in the Plate.

The Bend was rejected as a gimmick: a control whose only job was to make the object worse. Vigil
makes the model name load-bearing (Tenebrae is the rite of candles extinguished one by one), is a
genuine light-transport problem worth solving in WebGL, and cannot damage legibility because the
Screen lights itself.

**Consequences**: no visitor-caused corruption mechanic anywhere in the Unit. Do not reintroduce
`?bend=` or the melt shader from `/tmp/unit3d/scene.js`.
