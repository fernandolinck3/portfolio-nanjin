# Creative Brief — Fernando Portfolio

## Objective

Create Fernando's first portfolio from scratch. It should demonstrate his creative and technical potential while supporting conversations about job opportunities, freelance work, and relevant collaborations.

## Audience

- Employers and recruiters evaluating Fernando for frontend, marketing-adjacent, or emerging technology roles.
- Potential freelance clients looking for someone who combines creative judgment, communication, and frontend execution.

The portfolio is primarily written in English for an international audience.

## Core message

Fernando currently works with frontend development and a marketing perspective. He brings an artistic, culturally aware approach to technology and is actively building toward AI, business automation, and data analytics.

## Desired experience

The experience should feel like encountering an experimental creative studio while clearly meeting Fernando, the individual behind it.

It should progress emotionally from curiosity and atmosphere to comprehension, credibility, and an easy invitation to make contact.

## Communication principle

Essential claims, capabilities, and contact paths must be direct and conversational. Poetic language may create atmosphere and transitions, but must not replace meaning.

## Visual territory

Explore the tension between:

- Dream, memory, nostalgia, distortion, and cinematic emotion.
- Architectural order, typography, structure, and deliberate composition.

This territory must be translated into an original system. It must not reproduce the complete composition, branding, or signature device of any cultural reference.

## Proof constraint

Fernando has no existing case studies or completed AI, automation, or analytics projects. The finished portfolio will be the first public artifact.

Trust must come from the quality of the portfolio, honest positioning, visible thinking, and future self-initiated projects—not invented client history, testimonials, or metrics.

## Primary action

Encourage a visitor to contact Fernando about a job opportunity, freelance project, or collaboration. The final channel priority and CTA wording remain open.

## Experience constraints

- No personal photography is required.
- Avoid conventional SaaS visual language.
- Avoid generic portfolio templates and interchangeable project cards.
- Keep navigation, capabilities, authorship, and contact understandable even when the atmosphere is experimental.
- Choose technical complexity only when it strengthens the approved concept.

## Open items for later stages

- Approved creative direction.
- Page journey and content architecture.
- Exact project or process evidence to show at launch.
- Primary contact channel and CTA wording.
- Implementation stack and interaction medium.
