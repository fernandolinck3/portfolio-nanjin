# Page Plan — Memory Architecture

## Status

Proposed for approval. No implementation has started.

## Journey principle

The page moves from atmosphere to evidence. It begins with cultural and creative distinction, then steadily increases clarity until contact feels like the natural next passage.

## Persistent orientation

- **Visitor question:** Where am I, and can I navigate this unusual experience?
- **Content:** Fernando's name, section index, current section, and contact access.
- **Visual job:** Provide a stable architectural datum across changing compositions.
- **Interaction job:** Jump to a section and show location in the journey.
- **Proof:** None.
- **CTA:** Contact.
- **Medium:** HTML/CSS.
- **Acceptance:** Navigation is understandable by keyboard, pointer, and screen reader and remains usable without animation.

## 1. Threshold

- **Visitor question:** Who is Fernando, and why should I keep looking?
- **Content:** Fernando's name; a direct statement connecting frontend, marketing perspective, and creative technology; a concise indication that AI and automation are the direction being built toward.
- **Visual job:** Establish the contrast between a dreamlike image field and precise architectural information.
- **Interaction job:** A restrained framed reveal may expose layers without hiding the statement.
- **Proof:** The opening composition itself is the first signal of craft, not yet a claim of professional results.
- **CTA:** Enter Project 001 or continue exploring.
- **Medium:** React with semantic HTML/CSS. A contained R3F/WebGL memory window may be added after the static composition succeeds and must retain a CSS fallback.
- **Acceptance:** Name and current practice are understood within five seconds; the opening is asymmetric, legible, and recognizably not SaaS-like.

## 2. Present Coordinates

- **Visitor question:** What can Fernando actually contribute today?
- **Content:** Frontend development, marketing perspective, and the value of combining execution with communication.
- **Visual job:** Show two disciplines as connected working spaces rather than feature cards.
- **Interaction job:** None required; small annotations may reveal supporting detail.
- **Proof:** Specific decisions visible in this portfolio will be referenced where available.
- **CTA:** Continue to the project.
- **Medium:** HTML/CSS.
- **Acceptance:** Current capabilities are specific and never confused with the AI, automation, and analytics roadmap.

## 3. Project 001 — This Portfolio

- **Visitor question:** Can Fernando turn taste and reasoning into a functioning artifact?
- **Content:** The challenge, product truth, selected direction, meaningful design and frontend decisions, accessibility considerations, and final outcome.
- **Visual job:** Let the page expose part of its own architecture through annotated frames and before/after states created during implementation.
- **Interaction job:** Expand selected notes or move between a small number of process stages.
- **Proof:** Real screenshots, documented decisions, implementation details, and a link to source code if Fernando chooses to publish it.
- **CTA:** Inspect the process.
- **Medium:** HTML/CSS with optimized screenshots. Minimal DOM interaction; GSAP only if a transition communicates the process better than CSS.
- **Acceptance:** Every claim points to something actually visible or documented; no placeholder result is presented as completed work.

## 4. Working Method

- **Visitor question:** How does Fernando approach an unfamiliar problem?
- **Content:** Observe the context, define what is true, choose a direction, build deliberately, evaluate the result, and learn.
- **Visual job:** Turn the method into a traversable sequence of connected rooms or thresholds.
- **Interaction job:** Progressively reveal the relationship between the steps; the complete method remains readable without interaction.
- **Proof:** Link each step back to a real decision made while creating Project 001.
- **CTA:** None required.
- **Medium:** HTML/CSS. Optional restrained scroll animation after static review.
- **Acceptance:** The method feels personal and evidenced rather than like a generic agency process.

## 5. Current Horizon

- **Visitor question:** Where is Fernando going next, and is that relevant to me?
- **Content:** A clearly labeled learning and building direction covering AI for business, automation, and data analytics; a small queue of future self-initiated experiments only when honestly described as planned.
- **Visual job:** Open the rigid architecture into a wider, more speculative space without implying completed expertise.
- **Interaction job:** None.
- **Proof:** Learning notes or completed experiments may be added later; roadmap items remain labeled as roadmap.
- **CTA:** Propose a relevant opportunity or conversation.
- **Medium:** HTML/CSS.
- **Acceptance:** No reasonable visitor could mistake future interests for proven professional experience.

## 6. Open Passage

- **Visitor question:** How can I speak with Fernando?
- **Content:** A short personal invitation plus email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp.
- **Visual job:** Resolve the journey into the clearest and calmest space on the page.
- **Interaction job:** Copy email and open the selected contact channel.
- **Proof:** Direct access to Fernando.
- **CTA:** Start a conversation.
- **Medium:** HTML/CSS with minimal native interaction.
- **Acceptance:** Contact is obvious, keyboard accessible, and does not require a form or account.

## Implementation recommendation

Use Vite with React, semantic HTML, modern CSS, and TypeScript. React should organize real content and interaction rather than introduce unnecessary component abstraction.

The Threshold may use React Three Fiber for one contained WebGL memory window described in `SCENE-BRIEF.md`. Do not use WebGL for typography, navigation, or essential information, and do not add a large animation system for the initial section.

Build and visually review one section at a time. Establish the static composition and fallback before evaluating the enhanced scene. Project 001 evidence should be added only as it becomes real during the build.

## Approval gate

Implementation may begin only after Fernando approves this complete page journey.
